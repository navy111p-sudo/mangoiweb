MANGOi  ·  STUDENT LEARNING GAME

Avatar QuestLIVE

A companion that grows every time the student studies
3 August 2026 · Our 13th student game · Already available to students
Every speaking or vocabulary mission makes the student's companion grow, and at set points a short cinematic evolution plays. Yesterday's effort is still on the screen today — that is what gives the student a reason to come back.

1. Actual screens

Phone portrait view. Every label appears in both Korean and English.

Turn the phone sideways and the screen splits left and right — children often play games in landscape.

2. What the student does

STEP 1
Choose
Pick one of four. It starts as an egg.
STEP 2
Do missions
Speak aloud · match meaning · word order · listen & choose
STEP 3
Evolve
The egg hatches and a 3-second cinematic plays at each stage.
STEP 4
Weekly boss
Once a week, a 90-second challenge opens.
NOTE Why does “evolution” matter? Children respond far more strongly to a visible change than to a rising number. A score alone gets boring; a changing shape creates a reason to come back — “I want to see the next form.”

3. The companions

Everyone starts from an egg. Each egg carries a mark so you can tell what will hatch.

From left: panda (chef hat) · fox (stars) · dragon (scales and horns) · dog (cape band and star)

Missions make it grow. There are 4 species × 5 stages = 20 forms.

Each stage adds size and equipment

NOTE Why is only this one missing? It needs new storage and a new route on the server, and that work touches a core file every part of the service passes through. A mistake there could stop video classes and payments, so by our own rule only one designated maintainer may edit it. The screen side is already prepared, so it will connect as soon as that route exists. Students are not affected in the meantime.

6. Safety checks

PRIVACY No student's real name appears on the ranking. Only the nickname the student chose is shown, and if they have not chosen one, nothing is shown at all.
(A place where the real name would have been sent was found and fixed during development. Data was checked: zero students were actually exposed.)
NUMBERS Every figure on screen is a real record. If the server cannot be reached, the game does not invent a plausible number — it says “could not load right now”. Students who are not logged in always see “Guest mode — not saved”.
CHILD SAFETY The companion never dies or disappears, and points are never taken away for being absent. It only looks sleepy, and one mission restores it. We deliberately avoided “study or your character dies” pressure.
SPEAKING Saying “I have a cat” for “I have a dog” does not pass. A slightly shaky pronunciation does pass. We catch mistakes that change the meaning, not the child's accent.

8. Cost

Art and video
About 130 credits (17 AI videos + 24 images + 20 background removals)
Extra server cost
None — built by reusing what already existed
Student data use
About 0.3MB per clip, downloaded only when needed
NOTE The evolution clips are pre-made files. We do not call an AI each time a student evolves, so there is no recurring cost. Originals were 3–4MB each; we compressed them to one eighth of that, to a point where the difference is invisible on a phone. This was done with slower connections in the Philippines in mind.

9. Glossary

EXP
The score that makes the companion grow. It is the student's saved coin total on MANGOi, including coins earned in other games.
Evolution cinematic
The 3-second clip that plays when a stage is reached.
Deploy
Publishing a change to the live service, then checking the real address to confirm it is fine.
Guest mode
Not logged in. Playable, but nothing is saved — and the screen always says so.
